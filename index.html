Pleasant Yoga

Pleasant Yoga offers luxury, initimate retreats for those seeking a peaceful, restorative, functional approach to yoga.

My interest in yoga directly correlates with my experience as a young student at an acting conservatory in NYC. Those years immersed in theatre gave me a unique perspective into the inner workings of body mechanics. Constant adaptations to help create characters allowed me the opportunity to try on new physical characteristics. Spinal flexibility gave me creative license and sparked my curiosity about what keeps us upright. Studying the Alexander Technique, Joseph Pilates repertoire and the Feldenkrais Method grew my interest and laid the groundwork to become a Yoga Teacher.

Having taught thousands of public classes, I am more curious than ever! Every student I see has a story written in their bones and my deep desire is to be able to convey why some poses are more accessible and others might need adaptation. A functional anatomist to my core, my time in anatomy labs and studying fascial tensegrity continue to prove how unique our bodies are.

Most of my classes are breathcentric and designed to move Qi through their meridian pathways. Teaching acupressure techniques for healthy Qi movement is one of my passions and a key component of my class.

Whatever style of class I might be teaching, you can expect descriptive language and humor, powerful connection to your body, a safe place to feel vulnerable and the opportunity to create community.

If we feel alive in our body, we can laugh deeply, love generously and be fully present in the moment.

Pleasant Yoga is Tracy’s soulful passion project.

In a busy world of racing from work to mat to life, unplugging is the much needed escape we all crave.

From her background in hotel design to her creative endeavors, this is an opportunity for Tracy to blend her love of yoga, food, culture, wellness and community into an out of town, luxury experience. An opportunity for connection and an invitation to self care.
